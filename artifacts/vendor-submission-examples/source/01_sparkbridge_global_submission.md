Delays trigger same-day escalation with path-to-green options such as alternate edit routes, pre-cleared modular social assets, and staggered release priorities.

## Additional Strategy and Creative Response

Our strategy and creative approach starts with caregiver trust plus kid appeal, then builds one master campaign idea that travels from flagship film into social, retail, and launch governance assets. We would use modular creative components so claims-safe messaging can be adapted quickly without resetting the full approval cycle.
